# Neuro Desert — U01 opening, 0:00–0:15

## Storyboard

**Panel count: 7.** The panels lock three states of one continuous opening move, two distinct ownership-reveal states, Joe's activation key pose, and the final system state. They do **not** represent seven cuts.

### Shot 01 — 0:00–0:07 — one continuous move — panels 1–3

**Purpose:** Establish Joe, the autonomous private residence, and the root-replacement state without breaking room orientation.

| Panel | State | Framing / space / camera | Blocking and attention |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Start | Wide, Joe small left-of-centre in his rainy-night residence. Governance wall is physically integrated on the right wall; glass city wall lies behind/right. Camera looks diagonally across the room. | Joe faces screen-right toward the wall. Attention begins on the quiet room, then the wall's subdued overview. |
| 2 | Transition | Same shot: slow push-in with a slight lateral drift right. Joe moves into left foreground; wall gains dominance while the room geometry remains legible. | The overview opens a restrained **ROOT REPLACEMENT** detail. Joe makes only a minute focus shift. |
| 3 | End | Three-quarter over-the-shoulder, Joe in left foreground, governance wall on right. Preserve the same left-to-right viewing axis. | Hold the critical state: four signed authorizations, threshold met, objection window open. Joe is not shown inside the signer group. |

### Shot 02 — 0:07–0:11 — cut — panels 4–5

**Purpose:** Make the ownership implication legible: the threshold is already met, while Joe remains independent.

| Panel | State | Framing / space / camera | Blocking and attention |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | Signed-authority state | Clean interface insert of the same wall system, nearly frontal but slightly angled so it still reads as a surface. | Four signed trustee paths are complete. Joe's independent Emergency Reserve authority sits visibly outside the group. |
| 5 | Ownership reveal | Same insert and no new application. The four paths expand, trace, and converge. | The convergence ends at **SYMMETRY**. Attention travels from four separate paths to the single controlling entity; Joe remains separated. |

### Shot 03 — 0:11–0:15 — cut — panels 6–7

**Purpose:** Show Joe's deliberate intervention and the legally calm result.

| Panel | State | Framing / space / camera | Blocking and attention |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 6 | Activation key pose | Controlled medium near-profile/over-the-shoulder. Joe remains left; the same governance wall remains right. | Joe raises one hand toward the wall-anchored **EMERGENCY RESERVE** control. The gesture is precise and clearly physical. |
| 7 | Result state | Maintain the composition after contact; allow the wall state to settle rather than cutting again. | The interface shows **RESERVE ACTIVE** and **ROOT REPLACEMENT FROZEN**. Hold briefly; Joe is calm, with no celebration. |

## B — references + storyboard

```text
Create a 15-second 16:9 mature semi-realistic seinen sci-fi animation sequence.

Use all provided image references by role:
- Joe character master: identity authority.
- U01 autonomous residence master: architecture, room orientation, rainy-night atmosphere, and materials authority.
- Ownership-convergence UI reference: Shot 02 interface authority.
- Emergency Reserve UI reference: Shot 03 pre-activation authority.
- Reserve Active UI reference: Shot 03 final-state authority.
- Storyboard: directing authority only for shot order, framing, camera path, subject placement, screen direction, room orientation, key poses, cut points, and information timing.

The visual masters remain the final authorities for character, setting, interface design, colour, and rendering. Do not copy storyboard linework, labels, or rendering as final art.

Visual world: mature hard-sci-fi animation; semi-realistic anime character design; detailed 2D digitally painted backgrounds; controlled linework; elegant, institutional, high-end atmosphere. Keep it private, wealthy, quiet, and subtly threatening. Not photorealistic. Not live action. Not a 3D luxury real-estate render. No cyberpunk neon, game HUD aesthetics, or floating hologram clutter.

Character lock: Joe is a mature Central European man with gray swept-back hair, black rectangular glasses, light stubble, a slim elite build, and black near-future private workwear. Keep his face, age, hair, glasses, clothing identity, and restrained controlled presence consistent. No redesign.

Environment lock: rainy night; autonomous high-rise private residence; large intelligent glass wall facing the future city; governance wall integrated into the physical architecture; dark graphite and blue-gray palette; restrained warm amber practical light; expensive, understated residential atmosphere. Do not turn it into an office, command centre, or generic apartment. Do not move the governance wall.

Interface continuity: English only. One integrated Public Governance System on the physical governance wall; its shell, typography, spacing, and authority language stay consistent across shots. All UI stays anchored to the wall surface. Never generate detached, floating windows in front of Joe. The interface evolves: public governance overview → Root Replacement detail → ownership trace → Emergency Reserve → Reserve Active / Root Replacement Frozen.

Story causality: Four trustee authorizations are already signed and the replacement threshold is already met. Joe is separate from that signed-authority group and is not a pending fifth vote. He holds independent Emergency Reserve authority. The four apparently independent signed control paths converge on SYMMETRY. Joe recognizes this, deliberately activates Emergency Reserve, and freezes the Root Replacement during the objection window. Never portray the event as Joe refusing to approve a proposal.

Shot 01, 0:00–0:07, one continuous unbroken move: begin wide across the rainy residence with Joe left-of-centre and the governance wall on the right. Slowly push in with a slight lateral drift until a three-quarter over-the-shoulder composition puts Joe in the left foreground and the wall dominant on the right. Preserve room orientation and the left-to-right screen axis. Within the same wall system, a calm overview expands into Root Replacement detail. Prioritize readable meaning: ROOT REPLACEMENT, FOUR SIGNED AUTHORIZATIONS, THRESHOLD MET, OBJECTION WINDOW OPEN. Joe makes only a tiny focus shift.

Shot 02, 0:07–0:11, clean cut to an interface-focused insert of the same wall system: first show the four signed authorizations as complete and visibly separate from Joe's independent authority. Then expand the ownership trace so four paths converge to SYMMETRY, the strongest readable information. Keep it calm, legal, and institutional: no glitch, hacker effects, alarm graphics, or rapid zoom.

Shot 03, 0:11–0:15, clean cut back to a controlled medium over-the-shoulder or near-profile: Joe remains on the left and the same physical wall is on the right. Show EMERGENCY RESERVE and independent trustee authority. Joe makes one deliberate hand confirmation on the wall-integrated control. The system does not activate automatically. Let the same system shell settle into RESERVE ACTIVE and ROOT REPLACEMENT FROZEN, then hold the result briefly. Joe remains calm. No dialogue, no other characters, no exaggerated acting.

Performance and movement: subtle breathing, eye-focus changes, tiny posture adjustments, and one controlled activation gesture only. Use clean cuts between the three shots; no whip pans, handheld shake, orbiting camera, action-film movement, or flashy transitions.
```
